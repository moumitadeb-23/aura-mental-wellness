# Code of Conduct

We want AURA's community to be welcoming, respectful, and constructive.

Participants are expected to:

- Be respectful and patient.
- Discuss ideas without attacking people.
- Give useful, specific feedback.
- Avoid sharing private or sensitive information.
- Keep wellness-related discussions non-diagnostic and non-judgmental.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, or deliberate exposure of another person's private information are not acceptable.

Project maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when necessary to keep the community safe and constructive.
